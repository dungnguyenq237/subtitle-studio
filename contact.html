Questions, bugs or ideas?

Help improve Subtitle Studio by reporting a reproducible problem or suggesting a useful feature.

Contact the maintainer

This project is maintained through the Subtitle Studio GitHub repository. Use its Issues tab if available, or visit the maintainer's public profile for available contact options. A GitHub account may be required to participate.

What makes a helpful report?

  • The browser and device you used.
  • The steps you followed, what you expected and what happened.
  • A small, non-sensitive example that demonstrates the issue.

GitHub reports are public. Do not attach private files or personal data. We do not offer a guaranteed response time.

For data handling questions, read our privacy notice.
