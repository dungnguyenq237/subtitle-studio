Privacy, in plain language.

Your work stays on your device

The tool processes your input in your browser. We do not upload your files, text or generated output to an application server. Your input remains in the current page's memory and is cleared when you reload or close it. Downloads and PDFs you save remain on your device until you delete them.

Hosting and connection information

Render hosts the main website, and a copy may also be available on GitHub Pages. These providers receive connection information such as your IP address and browser request details when delivering the site. Their logging and retention are governed by their own policies. Fonts and application assets are served with the website.

Read the Render privacy policy and GitHub privacy statement.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

We currently run no analytics or advertising scripts, and the application does not set tracking cookies or save your input in browser storage. Advertising is disabled. If advertising is introduced, we will update this notice and provide the applicable privacy choices before enabling it. Uploaded text and generated content will not be passed as advertising targeting data.

External links and support

Following an external link takes you to another service with its own privacy rules. Support through GitHub is public: do not post private files, personal information or sensitive subtitle text. We do not have a server-side copy of your input to retrieve or delete.

Questions about this notice? Visit our contact page.
