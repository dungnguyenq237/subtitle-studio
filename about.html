A small studio for subtitle finishing.

Subtitle Studio is a free browser tool for shifting subtitle timestamps and converting between SubRip (SRT) and WebVTT (VTT). It is made for the final adjustments between an existing caption file and your next video edit.

What it does

  • Moves all cues earlier or later by a fixed number of seconds.
  • Converts SRT and VTT while retaining text and millisecond timing.
  • Flags overlapping cues and lines longer than 42 characters for review.

What to expect

This is not an automatic transcription, translation or speech-alignment service. It cannot infer the correct timing from a video. WebVTT positioning metadata is omitted when processing, so review styled or positioned captions carefully.

There is no account and no file upload to our server. Files are limited to 2 MB. The project is maintained at GitHub.
